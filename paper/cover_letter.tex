\opening{Dear Editor,}

We respectfully submit the manuscript ``Direction is geometric,
magnitude is not, and Bernoulli pressure drop alone deceives: a
four-variant clinical-quantity audit of graph neural network
surrogates for vascular blood flow'' for consideration at
\textit{Scientific Reports}.

\paragraph{The contribution.}
Graph neural network (GNN) surrogates for patient-specific
cardiovascular flow have advanced rapidly over the past two years,
with several recent papers reporting near-CFD accuracy on aggregate
velocity and wall-shear-stress metrics. The community has converged
on a small set of headline numbers without auditing which inputs
actually drive the prediction, and without examining whether the
clinical-summary scalars used to communicate results to clinicians
(notably the simplified-Bernoulli pressure drop $\Delta P = 4 v^2$)
are well-posed when the underlying surrogate field has degenerate
modes. Our manuscript fills both gaps with a single, reproducible
audit protocol.

We train four feature-leakage variants of the same GNN backbone
(direction leaked, magnitude leaked, both, or neither) on four flow
domains---patient-specific aortas (Vascular Model Repository),
analytical Womersley pipe, parametric Cosserat curved-tube sweep,
and synthetic U-bend CFD cases---giving roughly $200$ model--data
combinations. The same asymmetric pattern recurs in every domain:
the direction channel and the full-leakage variant achieve
indistinguishable angular accuracy (median $6^\circ$ on the U-bend
cohort, $n=75$ paired frame-cases; paired bootstrap CI on the
difference: $[-0.14, +0.13]^\circ$), while the magnitude channel
and the geometry-only variant remain near-random.

\paragraph{The methodological finding that we believe makes this
submission timely.}
On the U-bend cohort, the clinically prominent simplified-Bernoulli
pressure-drop MAE \emph{prefers} the geometry-only variant by
$3.55$~mmHg (95\% CI [$1.41, 5.57$]). At face value this would be a
strong endorsement of the geometry-only model. We show in
\textsc{Results} that this preference is an artefact of the
metric, not a feature of the model: the geometry-only variant
predicts a near-constant $\sim\!0.7$~mmHg pressure drop on every
frame, with per-frame Pearson correlation against ground truth of
$r=-0.002$ (statistically indistinguishable from zero on $n=225$
frames). The Bernoulli $\Delta P = 4 v^2$ reduction can therefore be
``won'' by a model that predicts nothing at all. We document the
mechanism, isolate the corresponding magnitude-collapse signature
in the velocity field, and propose that vascular-flow GNN
surrogate evaluations be reported on a clinical triplet
(pressure drop, wall shear stress, peak velocity localisation),
never on pressure drop alone.

\paragraph{Why \textit{Scientific Reports}.}
The audience for this work spans cardiovascular imaging, machine
learning for scientific computing, computational hemodynamics, and
the broader community concerned with reproducibility and audit
methodology in clinically-applied deep learning. The
broad-audience, methodologically rigorous, openly reproducible
profile of \textit{Scientific Reports} aligns directly with this
contribution. We release the four-variant audit pipeline (under a
permissive licence), all trained checkpoints, per-frame prediction
NPZs, paired-bootstrap CI code, and an SHA-256 reproducibility
manifest covering every artefact cited in the paper.
